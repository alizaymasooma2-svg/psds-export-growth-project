The dataset contains eight countries from 2001 to 2025. The selected 8 countries are Pakistan, the Philippines, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, South Africa, Morocco, and Ghana.
- country: The name of the country for each observation.
- year: The year to which the observation relates, covering 2001–2025.
- Export growth (NE.EXP.GNFS.KD.ZG): The annual percentage change in exports of goods and services, measured using constant 2015 US dollar prices.
- REER (PX.REX.REER): The real effective exchange rate index (2010 = 100), adjusted for relative price or cost changes across countries. An increase represents an appreciation of the local currency.
- GDP growth (NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG): The annual percentage change in GDP, measured using constant 2015 US dollar prices.
- Inflation (FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG): The annual percentage change in consumer prices, reflecting changes in the cost of a basket of goods and services.
- FDI net inflows (BX.KLT.DINV.WD.GD.ZS): Net foreign investment inflows, including new investments minus disinvestment, expressed as a percentage of GDP.
- Trade (NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS): The sum of exports and imports of goods and services, expressed as a percentage of GDP.
