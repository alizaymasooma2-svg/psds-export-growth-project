- We initially selected 20 countries from WDI which included South Asia, East and Southeast Asia, Latin America, Sub-Saharan Africa, the Middle East and North Africa, and Europe and Central Asia.

- Data availability issues: India, Bangladesh, Thailand, Indonesia, Viet Nam, Peru, Kenya, Egypt, Türkiye, and Argentina had no complete REER data for the selected period (2001–2025). China and Nigeria did not have complete data for annual export growth.

- Final selection: Due to these data gaps, we decided to drop the 12 countries and retained the remaining eight which are Pakistan, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, South Africa, the Philippines, Morocco, and Ghana with complete data availability for the indicators finalized. 

- Evidence for the change: The decision was based on checking the availability of the required WDI indicators across the selected years. The 12 excluded countries had incomplete data for at least one key variable, while the eight retained countries had the required data available.
